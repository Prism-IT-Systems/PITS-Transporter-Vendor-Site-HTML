Everything Your Diesel Fleet Needs

A comprehensive suite of tools designed to handle every aspect of diesel fuel purchasing, vehicle attribution, vendor invoices, and compliance auditing.

01

Create a Diesel Bill

Start by creating a new diesel bill and entering the basic purchase details.

  • Select diesel supplier and operating company
  • Enter invoice or slip number and billing period
  • Upload a digital copy of the original diesel bill or receipt
  • Set a common diesel rate to auto-apply to entries
02

Add Vehicle-Wise Diesel Entries

Add details for each vehicle that refueled under the supplier bill.

  • Select vehicle registration number and date
  • Enter diesel quantity in liters and rate per liter
  • Input odometer reading and optional operational notes
  • Use "Add Another Entry" for multiple fleet vehicles
03

Automatic Diesel Calculation

The system computes expenses automatically without manual calculations.

  • Formula: Diesel Amount = Liters × Rate per Liter
  • Example: 60 Liters × ₹90/L = ₹5,400
  • Aggregate total entries, liters, and bill amount
  • Update calculations in real time as quantities are entered
04

Save and Manage the Bill

Review the bill summary and save all vendor and vehicle logs in one click.

  • Click "Save All Logs" to save data securely
  • Review purchase details and attached slips anytime
  • Allow authorized users to add, update, or remove entries
  • Recalculate totals automatically after edits
05

Search, Track and Export Records

Access records from the Diesel Bills List or Vehicle Diesel Entries view.

  • Filter records by vendor, vehicle, invoice, or date range
  • Export complete datasets in CSV format for Excel or Tally
  • Share fuel expense statements with accounts teams and owners
  • Gain full visibility into fleet diesel expenditure
