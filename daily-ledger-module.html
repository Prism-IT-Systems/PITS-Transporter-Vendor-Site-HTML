Step 1: Review the Opening Balance

Assume the opening balance carried forward into this day is ₹5,000. It appears in the Opening Balance summary card before today’s entries are calculated.

Step 2: Add Credit / Received Entries

Under Credit / Received, enter a short description in Notes and the received Amount. Use + Add Row for each additional receipt.

Step 3: Add Debit / Payment Entries

Under Debit / Payment, select or enter the Payee, choose the Purpose where applicable, add Notes, select the payment Method, and enter the Amount. Use + Add Row to record multiple payments.

Step 4: Check the Automatic Closing Balance

The system updates the summary cards and the calculation bar using: Closing Balance = Opening Balance + Total Credit − Total Debit.

Step 5: Add Day Notes and Save

In Day Notes, enter an optional summary such as “Daily receipts and payments for driver, diesel vendor and vehicle expenses.” Review the entries and click Save Day Book. The save button may remain disabled when there are no unsaved changes.

Step 6: Open History or Another Day

Use History to review previous saved ledgers. Use Prev, Next or Date to move between days. Saved records can be reviewed and, where permitted, printed or exported.

Final result: the day begins with ₹5,000, receives ₹25,000 and records ₹20,500 in payments. The closing balance is ₹9,500.

Key Daily Ledger Features

Everything you need to record money received, payments made and the balance remaining each day.

Daily Ledger Records

Maintain a separate day-wise record of incoming and outgoing money for an individual user or manager.

Opening Balance

Start the day with an opening amount, including a suggested carry-forward from the previous available ledger.

Credit Transactions

Record money received, including manager advances that are synchronized from the advance records.

Debit Transactions

Record daily spending and payments made to the relevant party, driver, or for a vehicle-related purpose.

Vendor and Driver Payment Details

Choose a payee where applicable, add the amount and reference, and keep payment information linked to relevant records.

Multiple Payment Methods

Record payments made by cash, UPI, bank, or cheque.

Vehicle Expense Details

Add a vehicle for vehicle-related expense entries, helping connect expenses with the correct vehicle.

Automatic Totals

The system calculates total credits, total debits, and the remaining closing balance.

Ledger History and Editing

View saved ledgers and, where permitted, edit or delete them. Advance-linked credit rows are protected from ordinary row editing.

Manager Access Control

Permissions determine who can create, view, edit, or delete ledgers. Non-admin managers are restricted to their own records.

Print Daily Ledger

Open a printer-friendly daily ledger for review or physical filing.

CSV Export

Download daily ledger information as a CSV file containing totals and transaction entries for review in Excel.

Step-by-Step Workflow

How Does the Daily Ledger Module Work?

The module follows a simple process, from choosing the date to printing or exporting the ledger.

01

Choose the Manager and Date

Select the required user and ledger date. Administrators can access eligible users’ ledgers; a manager’s access is limited to their own ledger. Select the company when applicable.

02

Check the Opening Balance

The opening balance is money carried forward at the start of the day. The system can suggest it from the latest previous ledger’s closing balance.

03

Record Money Received (Credit)

Add a credit entry when funds are received. Manager advances recorded in the system can also appear as credit entries.

04

Record Expenses and Payments (Debit)

Add debit entries for money paid out: choose a vendor, driver, payment method, reference, amount and notes. Vehicle expenses may require a vehicle, and driver entries a purpose.

05

Review the Balance and Save

The system adds all credits and debits and calculates the day’s closing balance. Review the entries and save the ledger.

06

View, Print or Export

Open a saved ledger to review transactions. Use the print view for a paper copy or export the ledger as CSV for Excel.
