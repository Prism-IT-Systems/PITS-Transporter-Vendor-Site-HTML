Vehicle Document Module Features

Manage Vehicle Certificates, Track Expiry Dates & Keep Renewal Records in One Place.

Vehicle-Wise Document Records

Maintain documents under the correct vehicle number. Open a vehicle to see its related document information in one place.

Eight Supported Document Types

Manage RC, Fitness, Local Permit, National Permit, PUC, Tax, Insurance and Claim records. Each type can have its own document number and validity information.

Document Number and Validity Dates

Save the certificate or policy number, valid-from date and valid-to date so the document can be identified and its validity checked.

Expiry Status Tracking

The system marks documents as expired when their expiry date has passed and identifies documents due to expire within 30 days. This helps the team notice upcoming renewals.

Document Renewal

Renew an existing document by entering updated validity dates and, if available, a new file. Renewal creates a new record instead of replacing the old one.

Renewal History

Review older document records associated with a vehicle and document type. This provides a useful history of previous validity periods and attachments.

Digital Document Attachments

Upload a scan or photo of the document and access the file later using the available view or download options.

Insurance Information

For insurance documents, the module supports additional details such as premium amounts, zero-depreciation selection and premium owner, where relevant.

Document-Related Expense Details

For applicable non-insurance documents, record the vendor, expense amount, expense date and paid-by information to keep a reference to document-related costs.

Search and Document Type Filters

Look for a document using a vehicle number or filter the listing by document type. Vehicle-based grouping helps users browse different document categories.

View and Update Documents

Authorized users can review existing records and correct document details or attachments when information changes.

Permission-Controlled Access

Access to viewing, adding, editing and deleting document records is controlled through the application’s user permissions.

Step-by-Step Workflow

How Does the Vehicle Document Module Work?

Follow six simple steps to save a vehicle document, upload its file, and keep expiry dates and renewals under control.

01

Open Vehicle Documents

Go to the Vehicle Documents section to see saved records.

  • Search by vehicle number
  • Select a document type to narrow the list
02

Choose a Vehicle

Click Add Vehicle Document and select the vehicle for which you want to save the document.

  • Documents are saved under the correct vehicle
03

Select Document Type

Choose RC, Fitness, Local Permit, National Permit, PUC, Tax, Insurance or Claim.

  • Add more than one type while entering a vehicle’s documents when required
04

Enter Document Details

Enter the document number, starting date and expiry date.

  • Insurance information, vendor, expense amount or notes may be available depending on the type
05

Upload and Save

Attach a PDF or document image and save the details.

  • The new-document form accepts PDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG files
  • Up to 2 MB each
06

View Expiry and Renew

Review saved documents and their status.

  • When renewing, enter new validity dates and optionally upload a replacement file
  • The previous document stays available in renewal history

Permission-Controlled Access

Access to viewing, adding, editing and deleting document records is controlled through the application’s user permissions.
