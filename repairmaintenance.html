Repair & Maintenance Module

Managing repairs for multiple vehicles is easier when every workshop visit, part replacement and service expense is recorded in one system. The Repair & Maintenance Module helps transport and fleet businesses keep organized vehicle repair records instead of depending on scattered bills and handwritten notes.

Users can create a repair record for a vehicle, select the service vendor, record spare parts and labour work, upload the invoice and review previous repairs whenever needed.

Key Module Features

Everything You Need for Repair & Maintenance

Vehicle Repair Records

Keep a separate record of repair or maintenance work carried out on each vehicle, including the service date and a description of the work.

Workshop and Vendor Details

Select the workshop or service vendor responsible for the job. Vendor-based filtering helps review work completed by a specific supplier.

Spare Parts Management

Add multiple spare parts to one repair and record each part name, Company/Brand, warranty date, price and description. New Spare Part and Company/Brand values can be auto-saved to their selection lists and reused for future repairs.

Auto-Save of Spare Parts and Company/Brand: When users enter a new spare part name or company/brand through the supported add-new workflow, the system saves the new value for later selection in other repair records. This helps keep names consistent and reduces repeated typing.

Spare Part Warranty Date

Save a warranty date against a spare part when provided, so the information remains available in the repair details.

Labour Charge Management

Record mechanic or workshop labour charges separately from spare parts, with individual descriptions and amounts.

Repair Activity Description

Explain the work completed, making it easier to understand what was repaired when looking back at vehicle history.

Invoice Upload and Access

Attach a workshop invoice as a PDF or image. Saved invoices can be viewed or downloaded from the repair details.

Repair Expense Summary

View the recorded repair amount with a breakdown of spare parts and labour charges in the repair details.

Repair History and Details

Open an existing repair to view the vehicle, vendor, service information, spare parts, labour details and invoice together.

Edit and Delete Controls

Authorized users can correct existing repair information. Delete access is controlled separately by permissions.

Search, Filters and Sorting

Find repairs using vehicle number, vendor, vendor type, payment status and available sorting options.

Duplicate Repair Checking

A duplicate-check feature helps identify potentially repeated repair records during entry, reducing the chance of accidentally recording the same service twice.

Payment Status Information

Review and filter repair records by payment status where payment allocations are recorded in the system.

Step by Step

How Does the Repair & Maintenance Module Work?

01

Step 1: Add a Repair Record

Open Repair & Maintenance and select the option to add a repair.

  • Choose the vehicle and workshop/vendor, and enter the service date.
  • Upload the workshop invoice if available.
02

Step 2: Add Spare Parts

Add each replaced spare part with its part name, Company/Brand, warranty date (if available), amount and description.

  • Use Add More Spare to include several items.
  • The form also supports saving newly entered Spare Part names and Company/Brand names for future selection, reducing repeated manual entry.
03

Step 3: Add Labour Charges

Record the work performed by mechanics or the service provider.

  • Add a description and amount for each labour item.
  • Multiple labour entries can be recorded in one repair.
04

Step 4: Describe the Work and Check Total

Use Repair Activity Description to explain the repair or maintenance work, such as brake servicing or replacing a worn part.

  • Review the repair total shown on the form before saving.
05

Step 5: Save and Manage the Record

Save the repair and open it later to review the vehicle, workshop, part details, labour charges, repair amount and invoice.

  • Authorized users can edit or delete records when necessary.
06

Step 6: Search and Review Repair History

Use the Repair List to find previous work by vehicle, vendor and vendor type.

  • Payment-status filtering and sorting help users locate relevant records.
