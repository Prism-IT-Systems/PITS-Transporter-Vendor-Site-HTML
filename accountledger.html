Complete Solution

Everything Your Account Ledger Needs

A comprehensive suite of tools built to track vendor invoices, verify bank disbursements, audit driver payout histories, and eliminate bookkeeping confusion.

Vendor-Wise Account Ledger

View invoice and payment history for an individual supplier in a single ledger, helping staff understand bills received, amounts paid, and amounts still outstanding.

Multiple Vendor Categories

Choose from Business Vendors, Reefer/Cabin Vendors, and Office Vendors. Each selected supplier maintains its own distinct and isolated account ledger.

Driver Payment Ledger

Select an active driver to see recorded direct payments and the total paid over time. Drivers do not have an invoice-based payable balance in this ledger.

Invoice Transaction Records

Vendor ledger entries show invoices and related descriptions as payable amounts under Credit. These entries increase the supplier's outstanding balance.

Payment Transaction Records

Payments appear with useful details such as payment voucher number, company, payment method, and bank reference, reducing vendor payable balances.

Automatic Running Balance

The system automatically calculates the balance after each transaction (Balance = Total Invoices − Total Payments). For drivers, it displays cumulative total paid.

Ledger Summary

Quickly review vendor invoice totals, total payments, and closing balance, or review the total amount paid to a selected fleet driver at a glance.

Date-Wise Transaction History

Transactions appear in a date-based ledger, with the newest items shown first. Each row includes its transaction date, type, description, and running balance.

View Payment Details

Open linked payment records directly from the transaction list when a payment reference is available, providing fast access to supporting vouchers.

Simple Account Selection

Searchable selection controls help users pick a vendor or driver effortlessly without needing to memorize internal database IDs or accounting codes.

Access Control

Account ledger pages are available strictly to authenticated users with the required Payments View permission, keeping sensitive payout data secure.

Connection with Payment Records

The ledger brings together recorded vendor invoices and payments from across the accounting system, eliminating the need to manually maintain separate registers.

Step-by-Step Workflow

How Does the Account Ledger Module Work?

The module follows a simple five-step process that allows businesses to review supplier invoices, monitor payments, and check running balances without complicated calculations or paperwork.

01

Open Account Ledger

Go to Account Ledgers in the Accounts section. The page lets you select either a vendor or a driver. Only one selection is needed at a time.

  • Navigate to Accounts > Account Ledgers
  • Choose between Vendor Ledger or Driver Ledger view
  • Verified against user Payments View permission
Workflow Phase 01
02

Select a Vendor or Driver

For a vendor ledger, select a Business Vendor, Reefer/Cabin Vendor, or Office Vendor. For a driver ledger, select the required driver. Click Open Ledger.

  • Choose category: Business, Reefer, or Office Vendor
  • Search vendor name or select active fleet driver
  • Click "Open Ledger" to load the transaction ledger
Workflow Phase 02
03

Review the Summary

A vendor ledger displays Total Invoices / Payable, Total Payments and Closing Balance. A driver ledger displays Total Payments and Total Paid.

  • Vendor view: Total Invoices, Total Payments, Closing Balance
  • Driver view: Recorded payments count and cumulative amount paid
  • Instantly check account status before proceeding
Workflow Phase 03
04

Check Transaction History

The ledger lists transactions with date, type and description. Invoices increase payable dues; payments reduce them. The system computes a running balance.

  • Invoices appear under Credit (Payable)
  • Bank disbursements appear under Debit (Paid)
  • Newest entries displayed first with real-time balance
Workflow Phase 04
05

Open Related Payment Details

Where a payment is listed, open its linked payment details for reference. Use "Back to Ledgers" whenever you need to select another vendor or driver.

  • Click linked payment number to open full voucher
  • Inspect bank reference, payment method, and allocations
  • Seamless navigation back to other accounts
Workflow Phase 05
