Tyre Management Module

Managing tyres across multiple vehicles can be difficult when tyre numbers, purchase invoices, fitting dates and vehicle details are kept in separate registers. The Tyre Management Module keeps these records together in a simple digital system.

Users can record a new tyre, select the vehicle and supplier, enter the tyre type and size, save fitting details and store its purchase invoice. The module also makes it easy to search previous records and update incorrect information.

Key Module Features

Everything You Need for Tyre Management

4.1

Tyre Record Management

Add and maintain an individual record for each tyre. Each record brings vehicle, tyre identification, purchase, fitting and invoice information together.

4.2

Vehicle Assignment

Link a tyre to the vehicle where it is fitted. This helps users identify the vehicle associated with a recorded tyre.

4.3

New and Old Tyre Numbers

Save the current tyre number and, where relevant, the old tyre number being replaced. This provides a simple replacement reference without maintaining a separate replacement-history workflow.

4.4

Tyre Type Management

Add and maintain tyre types and choose the appropriate type when adding a tyre. The available tyre type list can be updated as business needs change.

4.5

Tyre Size Details

Record a tyre size to make it easier to identify the correct specification for a vehicle.

4.6

Vendor-Wise Purchase Details

Associate tyre purchases with a tyre supplier. This keeps supplier details connected to the relevant tyre records.

4.7

Fitting Date and Kilometre Reading

Record the date when a tyre was fitted and the vehicle kilometre reading at that time. These details can be referred to when reviewing fitting records.

4.8

Tyre Purchase Amount

Save the amount paid or billed for the tyre to keep its purchase cost with the record.

4.9

Digital Invoice Storage

Upload an invoice in PDF, JPG, JPEG or PNG format (up to 2 MB). When an invoice is available, authorized users can view or download it from the tyre details page.

4.10

Tyre Details and Editing

Open the details of a saved tyre and edit its vehicle, vendor, type, identifiers, size, amount, fitting information or invoice when changes are required.

4.11

Search, Sort and Payment Status Filters

Search tyre records by tyre number or vehicle number. Sort by fitting date or vendor and use payment status filters to narrow the list.

4.12

Access Permissions and Record Removal

Tyre viewing, creating, editing and deleting are controlled by user permissions. Authorized users can remove records when necessary.

Step by Step

How Does the Tyre Management Module Work?

01

Step 1: Open the Tyre Module

Open the Tyres section to view existing records.

  • Select Add Tyre to create a new entry.
02

Step 2: Select Vehicle and Vendor

Choose the vehicle on which the tyre is fitted.

  • Select the tyre supplier, if applicable, so the purchase can be linked to the correct vendor.
03

Step 3: Enter Tyre Information

Select a tyre type, enter the new tyre number and size, and record the old tyre number if an existing tyre is being replaced.

  • This keeps the identification details together.
04

Step 4: Record Fitting and Cost Details

Enter the fitting date, vehicle kilometre reading at fitting, and tyre purchase amount.

  • These details provide a useful reference for future checks.
05

Step 5: Upload Invoice and Save

Attach a photo or PDF of the purchase invoice if available.

  • Click Save Tyre to store the record.
06

Step 6: Find and Manage Records

Open the Tyres list to search by tyre number or vehicle number, view full details, check payment status filters, or edit an entry when permitted.
