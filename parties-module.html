Step-by-Step Workflow

How Does the Parties Management Module Work?

The module follows a simple process, from adding a party to using it in Lorry Receipts.

01

Open Parties Management

Open the Parties page to see the saved party list. Search by name or filter by party type to find a consignor or consignee.

02

Add a New Party

Choose Add Party and enter the party name. Select whether the party is a Consignor, Consignee, or Both.

03

Enter Contact Details

Add the phone number, email, GSTIN and complete address where available. These details can help identify the party when creating an LR.

04

Set Party Status

Choose Active for a party in regular use, or Inactive when the record should remain saved but is not currently in use.

05

Set Default LR Charges (When Enabled)

If contract charge settings are enabled in the company configuration, enter default amounts and notes for the available LR charge types.

06

Save and Use in Lorry Receipts

Save the party. During LR creation, select the saved consignor and consignee. Users with create permission can also quickly add a party from the LR form.

07

Find and Maintain Records

Search by party name, filter by type, open the party profile and make corrections. Authorized users may move a party to Trash and restore it later.
