Reefer Unit Work Management Module

Refrigerated transport vehicles use reefer units to help maintain suitable temperatures during journeys. When servicing, maintenance or replacement work is carried out on these units, businesses need to keep track of the work performed, the service provider, related expenses and warranty information.

The Reefer Unit Work Management Module makes it easy to record these jobs in one place. Users can link each work record to a vehicle, select the service vendor and company, save the expense, describe the work and attach invoice or warranty documents.

The module is useful for transport and fleet businesses managing refrigerated vehicles and their associated work records.

Key Module Features

Everything You Need for Reefer Unit Work

Vehicle-Wise Reefer Work Records

Link each repair or service record to a specific vehicle so you can review work done on that vehicle's refrigeration unit.

Reefer Service Vendor Selection

Select the Reefer/Cabin Vendor or workshop responsible for the work. Use a Reefer-type provider for refrigeration work, or a Reefer & Cabin provider when the same workshop handles both services. The selection comes from the separate reefer/cabin vendor directory.

Reefer Vendor Directory

Keep Reefer/Cabin Vendor names, contact numbers, email addresses and addresses in a shared service-provider directory, making workshop selection and follow-up easier.

Vendor Type Classification

The Reefer/Cabin Vendor directory supports three classifications: Reefer (refrigeration work), Cabin (cabin/body work), and Reefer & Cabin (both). Select the vendor that actually completed the job.

Company Association

Associate the work with the relevant company when the fleet is operated across multiple companies.

Work Date and Cost

Record when the work was performed and the amount charged so the business can review individual reefer work expenses.

Work Specification

Describe the actual work completed, such as checking the cooling unit, replacing a component or carrying out a repair.

Warranty Information

Save the warranty details supplied for the work or replacement parts. This is a text field for warranty information, not a confirmed automatic expiry-alert system.

Invoice Upload and Access

Attach a PDF or image of the vendor invoice to the work record and view or download it later.

Warranty Document Upload

Attach and access a separate warranty card or warranty document for the recorded work.

Document Updates

Authorized users can replace or remove stored invoice and warranty documents when required.

Search and Filters

Find work records by vehicle number or vendor name, and narrow the listing using the payment-status filter.

Sorting and Pagination

Browse records page by page and sort the listing by date or vendor.

Payment-Related Tracking

The module is connected to payment allocation functionality. Users can review available payment status information associated with work expenses, subject to the application's payment setup.

Duplicate Entry Warning

When the same vehicle, vendor, company and work date are entered again, the create flow can warn the user before allowing the record to be saved.

View, Edit and Delete

Open work records to review details and, with appropriate permissions, correct or remove records.

Step by Step

How Does the Reefer Unit Work Module Work?

01

Step 1: Open the Reefer Unit Work Section

Open the Reefer Unit Work list to view existing service and maintenance records.

  • You can search for a vehicle or vendor and open a record to see its details.
02

Step 2: Add a New Work Record

Choose Add Reefer Unit Work.

  • Select the vehicle and company, then choose the Reefer/Cabin Vendor responsible for the job.
  • The vendor directory groups service providers as Reefer, Cabin, or Reefer & Cabin.
  • Enter the date the work took place.
03

Step 3: Enter Work and Expense Details

Enter the work amount and describe the service, repair, replacement or other work in the Specification field.

  • Add warranty information when applicable.
04

Step 4: Upload Documents

Upload the related invoice and warranty document if you have them.

  • The form accepts PDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG files up to 2 MB per file.
05

Step 5: Save and Review

Save the record and open its detail page to review the vehicle, vendor, company, amount, warranty and documents.

  • The system can warn about a record with the same vehicle, vendor, company and date before saving.
06

Step 6: Find and Update Previous Work

Use vehicle number, vendor name and payment-status filtering to find records.

  • Authorized users can edit a record, replace documents or delete an entry when necessary.
