Step-by-Step Workflow

How Does the Payment Management Module Work?

The module follows a simple process, from creating a payment to exporting records.

01

Open Payments and Create a Record

Open the Payments section and choose to add a new payment. Select whether the payment is for a Vendor or a Driver, and enter the payment date.

02

Select Who Is Being Paid

Choose the vendor or driver. Vendor choices can include business, reefer/cabin and office vendors. Select the company or firm account making the payment.

03

Enter Payment Details

Select Cash, UPI, Bank, or Cheque. Add a reference such as a UTR, transaction ID, or cheque number, and include notes when needed.

04

Allocate Vendor Payments to Bills

The system loads the vendor’s unpaid invoices. Enter the amount paid against one or more invoices. The system checks that a payment is not allocated to another vendor’s bill or above the remaining balance.

05

Record Driver Payments

For a driver payment, enter the payment amount directly. This follows a different flow from allocating a vendor payment to invoices.

06

Save, Review and Export

Save the payment and open its details to check the payee, firm, method, reference, amount and linked allocations. Permitted users can edit or delete, search, filter and export as CSV.
