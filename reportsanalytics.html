Reports & Analytics Module

The Reports & Analytics Module helps businesses review important information recorded in different parts of the system. Instead of checking each diesel bill, vehicle expense, driver record, or vendor payment separately, users can open dedicated reports to find the information they need.

It is designed for fleet operators, transport businesses, accounts teams, and managers who need simple, organized views of operational records, expenses, document validity, and payments.

Key Module Features

Everything You Need for Reports & Analytics

Vehicle Reports

Select a vehicle to review its available operating records and expense-related information in a single report. The vehicle report includes relevant fleet categories and supports PDF and CSV export.

Category-Wise Vehicle Reports

When you need only one type of vehicle record, choose the vehicle and category. Available categories include Diesel, Tyre, Battery, Repair, Reefer Unit, Cabin/Body, Toll, Challan, Driver Pay, and Documents. Apply a date range where needed, then view or export the selected category report.

Vendor Reports

Select a vendor to review the records associated with that supplier. This helps businesses examine vendor-related activity without searching individual records across different sections. The vendor report includes PDF and CSV export options.

Driver Reports

Select a driver to view a report of the driver-related information available in the system. This helps managers and accounts teams review records associated with an individual driver. The report includes PDF and CSV export options.

Expiring and Expired Documents

Review documents that are approaching their expiry date or have already expired. The reporting area covers driver licences, vehicle documents, batteries, and office documents. Dedicated PDF downloads are available for the supported document groups.

Unpaid by Vendor

Use the Unpaid by Vendor report to review vendor-related unpaid amounts recorded by the system. It helps the accounts team identify supplier bills that still require payment attention.

Payments Report

View recorded payment information and use the available company and date filters to find relevant payments. This is useful for checking payment history and preparing internal accounts reviews.

Manager Float Report

Open the Manager Float report to review manager-related float records and balances presented by the system. This gives the business a dedicated place to examine manager float information.

Dashboard Overview

The dashboard shows counts of vehicles, drivers, vendors, and companies. It also displays recent diesel entries and document-related items requiring attention, including expiring or expired vehicle documents and relevant driver, battery, and office document dates.

Export Master Data

The Reports & Analytics page includes export options for Diesel Entries, Vehicles, Drivers, and Vendors. These downloads make it easier to keep copies of important records and work with them outside the application.

PDF and CSV Report Downloads

Supported detailed reports provide PDF or CSV downloads. PDF is useful for viewing or sharing a fixed-format report; CSV is useful when the information needs to be opened or examined in Excel. Export availability depends on the selected report.

Step by Step

How Does the Reports & Analytics Module Work?

01

Step 1: Open Reports & Analytics

Go to the Reports & Analytics page.

  • You will find separate options for Vehicle Reports, Vendor Reports, Driver Reports, Expiring Documents, Unpaid by Vendor, Payments, and Manager Float.
02

Step 2: Choose the Required Report

Select the type of information you want to check.

  • For example, choose Vehicle Reports to review one vehicle, Vendor Reports to examine a supplier, or Expiring Documents to see documents needing attention.
03

Step 3: Select the Record and Apply Filters

Choose an available vehicle, driver, or vendor, depending on the report.

  • Where date and other filters are provided, select the required values to narrow the report to relevant entries.
04

Step 4: Review the Results

The selected report displays related records and report details.

  • You can review purchases, expenses, payments, or expiry information according to the report selected.
05

Step 5: Download or Share the Results

Use PDF or CSV export on reports that provide those options.

  • CSV files can be opened in Excel, making it easier to use the data for internal checking and accounting.
