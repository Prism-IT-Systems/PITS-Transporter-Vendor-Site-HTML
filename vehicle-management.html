Vehicle Management Features

Manage Vehicle Details, Documents, Expiry Dates & Records in One Place.

Vehicle Registration and Profile

Create a separate record for each vehicle with its vehicle number, owner, company, model, year, chassis number, and engine number. Important identifiers such as the vehicle number, chassis number, and engine number are checked for duplicates when new records are created.

Owner and Company Information

Connect a vehicle with the company or firm that manages it, and maintain the owner name. This helps businesses see which vehicle belongs to which company record.

Vehicle Specifications

Record information such as the vehicle model, manufacturing year, gross vehicle weight (GVW), and unladen weight. This keeps basic identification and weight details available in the same vehicle profile.

Vehicle Documents Management

Maintain documents for RC, Fitness, Local Permit, National Permit, PUC, Tax, Insurance, and Claim. Document records can hold the document number, validity period, notes, and uploaded attachment. Some documents also support related expense or insurer information.

Document Expiry Tracking

Review the expiry date of each vehicle document. The documents listing identifies expired records and highlights documents that are nearing expiry, helping users plan renewals before documents become outdated.

Document Renewal and History

Renew an existing vehicle document by entering the new validity dates and, if needed, a new attachment. The previous record remains available in history instead of being overwritten, so users can review older document versions.

Insurance Details

Insurance-related records can include insurer/vendor information and policy-related financial details. Insurance data is maintained with the corresponding vehicle document, making it easier to review the policy record when needed.

VLTD Certificate Management

Record the Vehicle Location Tracking Device (VLTD) certificate number and upload the supporting certificate in PDF or image form. Where a certificate file is saved, the module provides ways to view or download it.

Search and Vehicle List

Search the Vehicles list by vehicle number to find the required record. The vehicle listing shows records in pages, making it easier to navigate when the business has many vehicles.

View, Edit and Status Management

Open a vehicle record to review saved information. Users with the required permissions can edit details and manage whether the vehicle is active. Deletion is also permission-controlled and should be used carefully because vehicle records may be connected to other business information.

Vehicle Documents Search and Filters

Use the documents page to find records by vehicle number and document type. Documents are grouped by vehicle so it is easier to review multiple document types for the same vehicle.

Connection with Diesel Records

Vehicles can be linked to Diesel Logs entries. Once a vehicle exists in the system, it can be selected when recording diesel purchases, helping maintain a consistent vehicle reference across modules. The Vehicle Module itself does not calculate diesel expenses; those are recorded in Diesel Logs.

Step-by-Step Workflow

How Does the Vehicle Module Work?

Follow six simple steps to add a vehicle, attach its documents, and keep expiry dates and renewals under control.

01

Open the Vehicles Page

Open Vehicles to view the existing vehicle list.

  • Look for a vehicle using its vehicle number
  • Open a vehicle to view its details
  • Choose Add Vehicle to register a new vehicle
02

Add Basic Vehicle Information

Enter the details that help identify the vehicle correctly.

  • Vehicle registration number, owner, and company (firm)
  • Model, manufacturing year, chassis number, and engine number
  • Optionally record gross vehicle weight (GVW) and unladen weight
  • Optionally add internal notes
03

Add Vehicle Certificates and Documents

Enter documents such as RC, Fitness, Local Permit, National Permit, PUC, Tax, Insurance, and Claim.

  • Record each document number and its validity dates
  • Upload a supporting file when available
  • RC information can also include its recorded address
04

Save the Vehicle

Review the entered information and save the record.

  • The system stores the vehicle profile
  • Associated document information is saved with it
  • Users can access the record later
05

View, Update and Maintain Records

Find the vehicle from the list, open its information, and make changes when required.

  • Authorized users can update vehicle details
  • Change the vehicle's active status
  • Manage the vehicle's documents
06

Check Document Expiry and Renewals

Use the Vehicle Documents section to check document validity.

  • Expired documents are identified
  • Documents with 30 days or less remaining can be highlighted
  • A renewal creates a new document record
  • The earlier record is preserved in history

Permission-Controlled Access

Users with the required permissions can edit vehicle details and manage whether a vehicle is active. Deletion is also permission-controlled and should be used carefully because vehicle records may be connected to other business information.

30 Days

Expiry Highlight Window

Documents approaching expiry can be highlighted when they have 30 days or less remaining, helping users plan renewals before documents become outdated.
