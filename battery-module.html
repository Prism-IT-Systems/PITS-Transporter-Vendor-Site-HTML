Battery Management Module Features

A comprehensive set of tools to record batteries, assign them to vehicles, track warranties, store documents and review payment information.

Battery Profile Management

Maintain an individual record for each battery with its battery number, type, cost, purchase date, expiry date and optional notes.

Vehicle-Wise Battery Assignment

Link a battery record to a specific vehicle so users can identify which vehicle it was purchased for and search records using the vehicle number.

Vendor and Company Information

Select the battery supplier and company for each record. Battery vendors are available from suppliers categorized under the Battery vendor type.

Purchase Cost Tracking

Record the battery purchase cost for financial reference and review payment information associated with the battery.

Warranty Period Tracking

The system derives a warranty period from the purchase and expiry dates, helping users understand how long the recorded warranty lasts.

Expired and Expiring Soon Status

Battery records can be marked as expired when the expiry date has passed, or expiring soon when the date falls within the next 30 days.

Invoice and Warranty Card Storage

Upload a battery invoice and a warranty card as separate files. When creating a record, PDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG files up to 2 MB each are accepted.

View and Download Documents

Open or download stored invoices and warranty cards from the battery record, subject to appropriate user access.

Battery List, Search and Filters

Search by battery number, vehicle number or vendor. The list also includes payment-status filtering, vendor sorting and paginated records.

View Complete Battery Details

Open any battery record to view the linked vehicle, supplier, company, dates, cost, warranty information and available documents.

Edit Battery Information

Authorized users can correct battery details, reassign the record to a different vehicle, update dates or costs, change notes and replace uploaded documents.

Duplicate Record Warning

During creation or editing, the system checks for an existing active or unexpired battery number and can show a warning before saving another matching record.

Payment Status and Allocations

The battery list displays payment status. Battery costs can also be connected with the application’s payment allocation features, depending on the configured payment workflow.

Permission-Based Record Management

Access to viewing, creating and editing battery records depends on the application’s user permissions. Battery records also support deletion by authorized actions.

Step-by-Step Workflow

How Does the Battery Management Module Work?

Keeping track of batteries across different vehicles is simple when purchase bills, warranty cards and expiry dates all live in one place.

01

Open the Battery List

Open the Battery Management section to see previously saved battery records.

  • The list shows key details for every battery
  • Use search and filters to find a record quickly
02

Add Battery Details

Click Add Battery and enter the basic battery information.

  • Enter the battery number and battery type
  • Enter the purchase cost
  • These details identify each battery and its expense
03

Choose Vehicle, Vendor and Company

Link the battery to the right vehicle and purchase source.

  • Select the vehicle using the battery
  • Select the supplier from whom it was purchased
  • Select the company responsible for the purchase
04

Enter Purchase and Warranty Dates

Add the dates that drive warranty tracking.

  • Add the purchase date and warranty expiry date
  • The system shows the warranty period
  • Expired and soon-to-expire warranties are identified
05

Upload Supporting Documents

Attach proof of purchase and warranty.

  • Attach the purchase invoice, if available
  • Attach the warranty card, if available
  • Add notes for future reference
06

Save and Manage the Battery

Save the record and manage it whenever needed.

  • The system checks for an existing battery number
  • A potential duplicate may trigger a warning
  • Authorized users can view, edit or delete the record later
