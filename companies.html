Complete solution

Companies Management Features

A set of tools to manage firm profiles, contact and tax details, addresses, status, and Lorry Receipt letterhead and numbering settings.

Company Profile Management

Create, view and update a company profile containing the firm name and business details. This keeps information for separate firms organized.

Contact Details

Maintain a contact person, primary phone number and email address. Additional phone numbers and email addresses can be stored for firm letterhead use.

GST and PAN Information

Save GST and PAN details to keep important business identifiers with the company profile.

Company Addresses

Maintain the main address and, when needed, separate office and residential addresses.

Active and Inactive Status

Mark a firm Active or Inactive to manage which company profiles are available in applicable parts of the application.

Company Logo and Signature

Upload or replace a company logo and signature image for company-branded documentation. Existing images can also be removed.

Lorry Receipt Letterhead

Configure company information used when preparing Lorry Receipts, including firm name, contact details, logo, signature, terms and jurisdiction text.

LR Numbering Settings

Set the LR number prefix and format, next sequence number and financial-year starting month for the selected firm.

LR Display Preferences

Choose whether the logo appears on an LR, adjust the firm name font size and control the contract-charges display preference.

Search and Browse Companies

Find a saved firm by name, use name suggestions and move through the company list with pagination.

Duplicate Company Warning

When a new or updated firm appears to share a phone number or GST number with an existing record, the system can warn the user and provide a link to view that firm.

View, Edit and Delete Permissions

Company management actions are permission-controlled. Authorized users can view, add, edit or delete firm records.

Step-by-Step Workflow

How Does the Companies Module Work?

Company details can also be selected in other parts of the system, such as vehicle and diesel bill records, so you only enter them once.

01

Open Companies

Go to Companies to see your registered firms.

  • Search by firm name
  • Check for an existing company before creating a new one
Workflow Phase 01
02

Add a New Company

Click Add New Firm and enter the required details.

  • Firm Name, Contact Person and Phone Number
  • Email Address, GST Number and Address
  • Required details are marked with an asterisk
Workflow Phase 02
03

Set Company Status

Choose whether the company is Active or Inactive.

  • Active firms are available for applicable selections within the application
Workflow Phase 03
04

Configure Firm Information

Complete the LR Letterhead section where needed.

  • Upload Logo and Operator Signature
  • Enter PAN, Jurisdiction Text, Residency Address and Office Address
  • Enter Letterhead Phone 1–3 and Letterhead Email 1–2
  • Leave the office address empty to use the main firm address
Workflow Phase 04
05

Set Up LR Letterhead

Configure numbering and display for Lorry Receipts.

  • In LR Numbering, select Prefix, Format, Next Sequence and FY Start Month
  • Choose whether to show contract charges and the company logo on the LR
  • Adjust the firm-name font size with the preview
  • Enter Terms & Conditions for printing on the LR
Workflow Phase 05
06

Save and Manage

Save the profile and manage it later.

  • Open it to review information and edit details
  • Search the company list
  • Delete a record if you have permission
