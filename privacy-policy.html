01
WELCOME

Introduction

At Transporter, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect information when you visit our website, use our platform, or interact with our transportation management services.

Your privacy is important to us.

We only collect information that is necessary to provide, improve, and secure our services.

02
INFORMATION

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our services, we may collect different types of information.

Personal Information

Name, email address, phone number, company details, and other information you provide when contacting us or creating an account.

Usage Information

Information about how you interact with our website, platform, features, and services.

Technical Information

Device information, browser type, IP address, operating system, and technical logs may be collected automatically.

03
PURPOSE

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and manage our transportation management services.

  • To communicate with you regarding your account, requests, or support inquiries.

  • To improve our platform, features, and overall user experience.

  • To monitor system performance and maintain platform security.

  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

04
TRANSPARENCY

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary to provide our services, operate our platform, protect our users, or meet legal obligations.

Service Providers

Trusted partners who help us operate and maintain our services.

Legal Requirements

When disclosure is required by law or valid legal process.

05
PROTECTION

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Security Comes First

We continuously work to maintain a secure and reliable environment for your transportation operations and data.

06
TRACKING

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website usage, remember preferences, and enhance your overall experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

07
YOUR CONTROL

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.

  • Request correction of inaccurate information.

  • Request deletion of your personal information where legally applicable.

  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

08
STORAGE

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, provide our services, resolve disputes, maintain business records, and comply with legal obligations.

09
UPDATES

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with the revised effective date.
