01. Introduction

FleetWave ("we," "our," or "us") provides a transportation and fleet management platform that helps businesses manage vehicles, drivers, diesel logs, maintenance, tolls, and day-to-day operations. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website and platform, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using FleetWave, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not access or use our services.

02. Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to operate the platform and deliver fleet management features to your organization.

Account & Profile

Name, work email, phone number, company details, and role permissions.

Fleet & Vehicle Data

Vehicle documents, RC/permits, diesel logs, maintenance records, and toll history.

Location & Tracking

Real-time GPS coordinates and route data when live tracking is enabled by your organization.

Usage & Device Data

Log-in activity, IP address, browser type, and interactions with the dashboard.

03. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to operate, secure, and improve FleetWave, specifically to:

  • Provide core features such as vehicle tracking, driver management, diesel logs, and reporting.
  • Send maintenance reminders, document expiry alerts, and compliance notifications.
  • Generate analytics and reports that support fleet and cost optimization decisions.
  • Process payments to drivers, vendors, and toll or challan authorities on your behalf.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Communicate product updates, service changes, and support responses.

04. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal or fleet data. Information is only shared in the following circumstances:

  • Within your organization — with fleet managers and other users your admin grants access to, according to their permission level.
  • Service providers — payment processors, cloud hosting, and analytics vendors bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements — where disclosure is required to comply with law, regulation, or a valid legal process.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice provided in advance.

05. Data Security

We apply enterprise-grade safeguards to protect fleet and account information, including encrypted data storage, secure user authentication, and controlled system access based on role permissions. Access to sensitive records such as driver payments and compliance documents is restricted and logged.

While we work to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We encourage strong, unique passwords and prompt reporting of any suspected unauthorized access.

06. Data Retention

We retain account, vehicle, and operational data for as long as your organization maintains an active FleetWave subscription, and for a limited period afterward to meet legal, tax, and compliance obligations. Data tied to regulatory records, such as permits and challans, may be retained longer where required by applicable law.

07. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or export a copy of your personal data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing, including analytics.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact your organization's fleet administrator or reach us directly at privacy@fleetwave.io.

08. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember dashboard preferences, and understand how the platform is used so we can improve it. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may limit certain features.

09. Children's Privacy

FleetWave is a business platform intended for use by transportation and logistics professionals. It is not directed at individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, including India, where our infrastructure and support teams are based. Where required, we rely on appropriate legal safeguards to protect data transferred across borders.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Material changes will be communicated by email or an in-app notice, and the "Last updated" date above will always reflect the most recent revision.
