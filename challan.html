Complete solution

Everything Your Challans Need

A set of tools to record challans against vehicles and drivers, attach slips, review duplicates and find records quickly.

Central Challan Records

Keep every traffic challan recorded in one place, so paper slips are not misplaced and previous records are always available on the Challans page.

Vehicle Selection

Select the vehicle for which the challan was issued from the available vehicle list.

Driver Selection

Select the related driver from the available driver list, making it easier to identify who was involved.

Challan Information

Enter the challan number, challan date, fine amount and the reason for the challan to identify what happened and when.

Digital Challan Slip Upload

Upload a digital challan slip as a PDF or image. The current form supports JPG, JPEG, PNG and PDF files up to 2 MB.

Duplicate Challan Warning

If a similar challan is already saved, the system can show a warning and a link to the existing record.

Save Anyway Decision

Review the existing record first, then decide whether to use Save Anyway.

View or Download Attachment

Open the saved challan and view or download its attached slip whenever it is needed.

Search Challans

Search records by challan number, vehicle or driver to find a challan quickly.

Edit Records

Authorized users can open a challan’s details and edit the record when a correction is needed.

Step-by-Step Workflow

How Does the Challan Module Work?

Follow a simple process to record a challan, attach its slip, check for duplicates and manage it later.

01

Open the Challans Section

Open the Challans page to see previously recorded challans.

  • Open the module from the dashboard sidebar
  • Review the saved challans on the list page
  • Select Add Challan to create a new record
02

Choose Vehicle and Driver

Link the challan to the right vehicle and driver.

  • Select the vehicle for which the challan was issued
  • Select the related driver from the available lists
03

Enter Challan Information

Record the details that describe the challan.

  • Enter the challan number and challan date
  • Enter the fine amount
  • Enter the reason for the challan
04

Attach the Challan Slip

Upload a digital copy of the slip, if you have one.

  • Upload a PDF or image of the challan slip
  • JPG, JPEG, PNG and PDF files up to 2 MB are supported
05

Review Duplicate Warning

Check whether a similar challan already exists.

  • The system can show a warning for a similar saved challan
  • A link to the existing record is provided
  • Review it before deciding whether to use Save Anyway
06

Save and Manage

Save the challan and manage it whenever needed.

  • Open its details after saving
  • View or download the attachment
  • Search by challan number, vehicle or driver
  • Edit records when authorized
