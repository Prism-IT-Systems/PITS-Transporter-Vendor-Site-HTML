Cabin Body Work Module Features

A comprehensive set of tools to record cabin and body work, choose workshops, track repair costs and find past entries quickly.

Vehicle-Wise Work Records

Connect every cabin or body work entry to a specific vehicle, so it is easier to check what work has been recorded for that vehicle.

Workshop / Vendor Selection

Select the workshop from the Reefer/Cabin Vendor directory. For a cabin/body job, choose a Cabin vendor or a Reefer & Cabin vendor that provides both services.

Vendor Type Information

Each Reefer/Cabin Vendor can be classified as Reefer, Cabin, or Reefer & Cabin. The selected vendor type helps users understand the service provider’s work category.

Work Date Recording

Save the date when the cabin or body work took place to keep a clear chronological history.

Repair and Service Cost

Enter the amount charged for each job, so the business can review the recorded cost of individual repairs or modifications.

Work Specification

Describe the work performed in plain language, such as door repair, body panel replacement, cabin painting or structural maintenance.

Work Detail View

Open a saved record to view its vehicle number, vendor details, work date, amount and specification together.

Vehicle Search

Find matching cabin and body work entries by entering a vehicle number.

Vendor Search

Find work associated with a particular vendor using vendor-name search and suggestions.

Payment Status Filter

The listing includes an existing payment-status filter. Its results depend on payment information configured elsewhere in the application; this module does not itself provide a dedicated payment-entry form.

Sorting and Pagination

Browse the work list page by page, with sorting available by date or vendor.

Edit Existing Records

Authorized users can correct vehicle, vendor, work date, amount or specification information.

Delete Records

Users with delete permission can remove an incorrect or unnecessary work record.

Role-Based Access

The application checks permissions before allowing users to view, create, edit, update or delete cabin body work records.

Step-by-Step Workflow

How Does the Cabin Body Work Module Work?

Vehicle cabins and bodies may need repairs, modifications or other workshop work over time. A written record of each job, the workshop involved and its cost keeps that history clear.

01

Open Cabin Body Work

Open the Cabin Body Work section to see previously recorded work.

  • Review the listing of recorded work
  • Find a record using its vehicle number or vendor name
02

Add a New Work Record

Choose the option to add cabin body work.

  • Select the vehicle that received the work
  • The work can be a repair or other body-related service
03

Select Vendor and Work Date

Choose the workshop that completed the job and enter the date.

  • Choose the Reefer/Cabin Vendor or workshop
  • Vendors are identified as Cabin, Reefer, or Reefer & Cabin
  • Enter the work date
04

Enter Amount and Work Description

Record what was charged and what was done.

  • Enter the amount charged for the work
  • Use the Specification field for a brief description
  • Describe the repair, replacement, painting or other work
05

Save and Review

Save the record to add it to the list.

  • The work appears in the Cabin Body Work list
  • The detail page shows the vehicle, vendor, amount and specification
06

Search and Update

Find earlier records whenever you need them.

  • Search by vehicle or vendor
  • Authorized users can edit incorrect details
  • Authorized users can delete an entry when necessary
