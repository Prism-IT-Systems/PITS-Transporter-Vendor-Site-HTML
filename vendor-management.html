Vendor Management Features

Manage Supplier Profiles, Vendor Types, Contacts and Payment Information in One Place.

Vendor Profile Management

Store essential supplier details such as vendor name, contact person, phone, email, GST number and address. Staff can retrieve the information from a single saved profile.

Multiple Vendor Categories

Classify a supplier under one or more supported types: Tyre, Battery, Diesel, Fuel Card, Repairs, RTO, Insurance, Refrigeration and Toll. This makes it easier to identify the right supplier for different needs.

Vendor Contact Information

Keep the name of the contact person along with available phone, email and business address details, helping the team contact suppliers without checking separate records.

GST Information

Record a supplier GST number when available. This provides a useful reference for invoice and accounts-related work.

Active and Inactive Vendor Status

Mark vendors Active or Inactive depending on whether they are currently used. The profile keeps the status alongside other vendor information.

Vendor List and Search

Browse a paginated vendor list and search by vendor name. The interface also offers name suggestions while searching.

Filter by Vendor Type

Filter the vendor list to find suppliers from a particular category, such as Diesel, Tyre or Insurance.

Sorting and Browsing

Sort the list by vendor name or browse recently created records to find suppliers more quickly.

Edit Vendor Details

Authorized users can update supplier contact details, vendor categories, GST number, business address and active status as information changes.

Duplicate Vendor Check

When adding or updating a supplier, the system checks for possible duplicates using details such as vendor name, phone and GST number, and can show a warning before a user proceeds.

Vendor Payment Overview

The vendor detail page shows recent vendor-linked payments and outstanding payable information when records exist. Payments themselves are managed through the application’s separate payment workflow.

Permission-Based Vendor Actions

Viewing, creating, editing and deleting vendor records are controlled by the application’s permissions, so staff can access only the actions allowed to them.
